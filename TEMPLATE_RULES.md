# Newsletter Template Rules

## Layout & Structure
- **Overall Width:** Main sections (This Month, Big News, Into, Dates, Shoutouts) should be centered with a `max-width` of `900px`.
- **Opener Section:** Should be wider than the standard sections to create a more open feel. Use a `max-width` of at least `1200px` or `100%` with generous padding.
- **Featured Cards:** In the "This Month" section, the first card (Kiara) is "featured" and should span the full width of the grid (`grid-column: 1 / -1`).
- **Standard Cards:** Remaining cards should follow in a 2-column grid (2x2 layout).

## Key Features
- **Hero Typewriter:** The hero title should have a typewriter animation.
- **Sticky Nav:** A navigation bar should appear at the top after scrolling past the hero title.
- **Issue Dropdown:** The "April 2026" (or current month) text in the hero should be a clickable button with a chevron that reveals a dropdown for past issues.
- **Countdown Bar:** A fixed bar at the top (below the preview banner if present) showing the next upcoming birthday.
- **Interactive Calendar:** A calendar that allows navigating months and highlights birthdays with initials in dots.
- **Submit/Contact:** A "Text me" button at the bottom that opens an SMS link.

## Typography & Style
- **Fonts:** Georgia/Serif for body and titles, Helvetica/Sans-serif for labels and metadata.
- **Drop Cap:** The first letter of the opener should be a large drop cap.
- **Italics:** Use italics for friend updates and commentary to differentiate from labels.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Ink: `#111110`
  - Paper: `#faf9f6` (Warm variant: `#f2f0ea`)
  - Rule: `#e0ddd5`
